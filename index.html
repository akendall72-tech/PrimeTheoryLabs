How we work

Built on first principles

Four steps, in order, every time.

  1. Define

    Who uses the app, what they need to get done, and what "done" looks like. Written down before anything is built.

  2. Design

    Screens and flows you can tap through on your own phone before production code starts.

  3. Build

    Native Swift and SwiftUI, tested on real devices, with TestFlight builds so you see progress as it happens.

  4. Ship

    App Store submission handled for you, then updates as your needs and iOS change.
